# Supply and Demand Indicator for TradingView

A custom **Pine Script** indicator designed for TradingView to automatically identify and plot key supply and demand zones on your charts.

## 🚀 How to Install and Use

Follow these simple steps to add the indicator to your TradingView charts:

1. **Copy the Code:** Open the indicator file SupplyAndDemandZones.pine from the dist folder in this repository and copy the entire script.
2. **Open TradingView:** Go to [TradingView](https://tradingview.com) and open any asset chart.
3. **Open Pine Editor:** At the bottom of the screen, click on the **Pine Editor** tab.
4. **Paste the Code:** Clear any default code currently in the editor, and paste the copied script.
5. **Save the Indicator:**
   - Click the **Save** button in the top right corner of the Pine Editor.
   - Give your indicator a name (e.g., "Supply and Demand Zones").
6. **Add to Chart:** Click **Add to chart** right next to the Save button.

---

## 🛠️ Configuration & Settings

Once added, you can customize the indicator behavior to match your trading style:

- Hover over the indicator name on your chart and click the **Gear Icon (Settings)**.
- Adjust zone colors, line color, and zone size under the **Inputs** and **Style** tabs.

## 📌 Features

- **Automated Zone Detection:** Instantly identifies supply and demand areas.
- **Customization:** Easily change colors for supply (resistance) and demand (support) blocks.
- **Real-time Updates:** Zones dynamically adjust based on the selected timeframe.
